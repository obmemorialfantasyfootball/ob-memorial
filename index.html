How to Play

Pick one choice from each of Groups 1-14: 2 QBs, 4 RBs, 4 WRs, 1 TE, 1 kicker, 1 NFC D/ST and 1 AFC D/ST. Only teams playing Sunday at 1:00 PM ET or later are eligible. Weekly player pools are set when the sheet is created; if a listed player later becomes unavailable, the selection stands.

Extra Points & D/ST

PAT: 1 point. Successful 2-point conversion by rush, reception or pass: 4 points. D/ST return TDs use the distance table above. Safety: 5 points. Shutout: 10 points.

Weekly Bonus

Predict the combined final score of the designated Sunday game. A prediction within 7 points above or below the actual combined score earns 5 bonus points.
