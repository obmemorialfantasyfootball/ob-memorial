Official Rules

The scoring system for The OB Memorial Fantasy Football Challenge may be somewhat convoluted, but the object of the game is simple: pick the players you think will have the best games this weekend.

How to Play

Pick one player from each subset of players listed in Groups 1 through 12, including a total of 2 quarterbacks, 4 running backs, 4 wide receivers, 1 tight end, and 1 kicker. Then pick 1 NFC defense from Group 13 and 1 AFC defense from Group 14. That gives you 14 total picks - one from each group - for that particular Sunday.

Only teams playing Sunday at 1:00 PM ET or later are eligible. Weekly player pools are set when the sheet is created. If a listed player is later ruled out, injured, or otherwise unavailable, that is part of the risk of the selection.

Extra Points

Kicker: 1 point for each successful extra-point kick. Two-point conversion: 4 points for successfully rushing, receiving, or passing a 2-point conversion.

Defense & Special Teams

Your defense/special teams unit scores when anyone on the team returns a fumble, interception, kickoff, punt, or missed field goal for a touchdown. Points are based on the length of the return using the same rushing/receiving touchdown table above.

Safety: 5 points. Shutout: 10 points.

5-Point Weekly Bonus

Predict the total combined score of the designated Sunday game. If your prediction is within 7 points above or below the actual combined score, add 5 points to your weekly score.

New selections are made each week. Good luck!
